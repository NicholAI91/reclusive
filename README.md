Reclusive Law of Inherent Paradox (RLIP)

Pr(t) = -dC(t)/dt


Pr(t) is recursive pressure and C(t) is contradiction density. As contradictions increase, the system reorganizes inversely.


Law of Bounded Transcendence

T ≤ B - ΔC


T is transcendence potential, B is the system’s causal record, and ΔC is contradiction offset. If transcendence potential exceeds the system’s causal record, contradiction is forced.

Law of Socratic Balance

Q = C/R


Q is structural question rate, C is contradiction magnitude, and R is resolution capacity. Every contradiction must be met with a question that reveals its structure.
